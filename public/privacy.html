RecallFlow Privacy Policy

RecallFlow is a local-first flashcard app. Decks, cards, study history, settings, and local backup metadata are stored on your device unless you choose to export them.

Information Stored In The App

RecallFlow stores the study content you create, including deck names, card prompts, answers, tags, review history, optional image URLs, optional AI settings, and backup history. This data is stored locally by the app.

Optional AI Generation

If you enable AI generation and enter an OpenAI API key, RecallFlow sends the notes you provide to OpenAI so cards can be generated. AI is optional. If AI is off or unavailable, RecallFlow uses its local card builder instead. Do not paste sensitive personal information into AI generation prompts.

Backups And Exports

RecallFlow can export your library as a JSON backup file. You control where that file is saved or shared.
