Common Questions

Where is my data stored?
RecallFlow stores decks, cards, settings, and study history locally on your device.

Do I need AI to use RecallFlow?
No. AI generation is optional. The local builder works without an API key.

How do I back up my cards?
Open the Build tab, then use Export backup to save a JSON copy of your library.
